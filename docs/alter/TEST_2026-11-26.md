## Finding 2: the draft comes back about 7% fast (VERIFIED)

The raw file is 89 frames at 24 fps (3.708 s) for a 96-frame, 4.000 s source. Scored as delivered, his mouth reads 0.16 on the take's clock and the gate fails it as re-timed. That reading is wrong about the cause:

| How the draft is laid against the take | Mouth timing |
|---|---|
| As delivered, frame for frame | 0.146 |
| Shifted, best constant offset (4 frames) | 0.661 |
| Stretched evenly over the take (89 frames onto 0 to 94.5) | 0.901 |

The whole performance is there, played about 7% fast. This is the same behaviour as Genjutsu (`scripts/edit/swap_rate.py`: "89 frames for 4.004 s"), and the same cure: bring the clip to the source's length, then interpolate to the source's rate. Done here with `setpts=PTS*96/89` and the `swap_rate.py` interpolation filter, padded to exactly 96 frames.

**A draft must be conformed to the take's length before it is scored.** The checker's "length" issue is the signal; the mouth gate's "re-timed" verdict on an unconformed draft is not evidence.
