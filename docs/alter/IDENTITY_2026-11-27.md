## What the readings say

- VERIFIED: an edit that keeps his face reads close to 0 against its own take (0.021), as does the take's own small copy.
- VERIFIED: the number does not see a face that slides. The put-back he failed reads *closer* (0.010) than the draft he
  passed. It measures who the face is, not whether it holds still. A face-steadiness measure is still missing.
- VERIFIED: his own face on other takes (other cap, outfit, light, distance) reads 0.21 to 0.30 from this take. The inherited
  0.25 line (`camera_coverage.py` `identity_max`) therefore sits inside the range of Fendi against Fendi across takes:
  a redraw could move as far from the take as he looks from one take to another and still be under it.
- NOT MEASURED: a face he rejected ("the person was clearly not me in the face", Hyenas, 12 Nov), any other person, a
  generated lookalike. Without one there is no evidence for where "not him" starts.
- OBSERVED: all faces here are small (a 480p draft, a waist-up frame). HYPOTHESIS: small faces compress the distances;
  test by scoring the same pair from a 4K face crop.

## Decision

DECISION (27 Nov 2026): the identity line is **not certified** (`docs/REPRODUCIBLE_BENCHMARK_SYSTEM.md`: a gate is
derived from evidence, never recalled). In `judge.ts`:

- a number **above** 0.25 still fails the draft ("The face is not his"): that is about as far from the take as he is from
  himself on another take, or further;
- a number **under** it clears nothing. The draft is CHECK with `faceNeedsHisEye`, the full-resolution plan still needs
  `face_approval` (his own words about that draft's face), and the tool says the line is not certified.

Before this change a number under 0.25 set `redrawCleared` and built the full-resolution call without his words, on a
line nobody had measured.

## To certify the line later

1. Decide the model (licence above).
2. Collect, through the same steps (`run_draft.py`): redraws of his take that he passed, redraws he rejected for the
   face, and at least one other person in a comparable frame.
3. Derive the line from those scores with the margins shown, present it to Fendi, freeze it with provenance, and only
   then set `IDENTITY_GATE_CERTIFIED` (Class C, evidence in the same PR).
