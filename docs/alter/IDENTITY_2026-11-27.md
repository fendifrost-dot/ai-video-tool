## Decision

DECISION (27 Nov 2026): the identity line is **not certified** (`docs/REPRODUCIBLE_BENCHMARK_SYSTEM.md`: a gate is
derived from evidence, never recalled). In `judge.ts`:

- a number **above** 0.25 still fails the draft, and his words cannot override it in the tool. This is a DECISION that
  fails closed, not a measured boundary: against its own take a kept face read 0.000 to 0.021, so 0.25 on the same
  frames is a large move, but the line itself is as uncertified on this side as on the other. The cost of being wrong
  is a re-roll. If he passes by eye a draft the tool failed on this line, that is the first evidence to move it;
- a number **under** it clears nothing. The draft is CHECK with `faceNeedsHisEye`, the full-resolution plan still needs
  `face_approval` (his own words about that draft's face), and the tool says the line is not certified.

Before this change a number under 0.25 set `redrawCleared` and built the full-resolution call without his words, on a
line nobody had measured.

## The test group: built inside Meditate, while the video is built

Fendi, 27 Nov 2026: "The fail pass needs another methodology or test group which can be done inside meditate because going back to older projects is counterproductive. I'm working on meditate now and the same way we have tested while building in previous sessions we can do the same here".

DECISION (his): the pass/fail line is not derived by going back to older projects. Every draft he judges on Meditate
goes into a ledger with its numbers and his words, and a line is read from that group when it is big enough.

**The ledger:** `/Volumes/T7/_avt_work/alter_face_evidence/meditate.jsonl`, written by `scripts/alter/face_evidence.py`
(method `face_evidence/1`). It holds numbers, labels and his words: no picture, no link. One line per draft he judged.

**Three measures per draft** (higher is worse on all three). Each is judged only against the fault it is about:

| Measure | What it is | Judged against | The draft he passed | The put-back he failed | Control: the take against its own 640-wide copy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `identity_distance` | who the face is: distance between the mean face embedding of the take and of the draft | faces rejected as not him (`--fault who`) | 0.021 | 0.010 | 0.000 |
| `embedding_jitter` | how much the face embedding changes from frame to frame in the draft, over the same on the take | faces rejected as warping or sliding (`--fault steady`) | 0.766 | 1.581 | 0.896 |
| `shape_wobble` | how far a frame's face outline sits from where its two neighbours put it, each fitted onto it by shift, turn and scale; over the same on the take | `--fault steady` | 0.883 | 1.219 | 1.098 |

- VERIFIED (these three rows, all Meditate `IMG_6550` 0:40-0:44): both steadiness measures read the failed put-back
  above his real take and the passed draft under it. Identity distance reads the put-back closer than the passed draft,
  which is expected (the put-back is his own pixels, failed for warping) and says nothing against identity as a check
  of who the face is.
- OBSERVED: the control shows what size alone does. The same take at 640 wide reads 0.90 on jitter and 1.10 on wobble,
  so about a tenth either way is the method's own noise here. Jitter's gap (0.77 against 1.58) is well outside it;
  wobble's (0.88 against 1.22) is not far outside it.
- HYPOTHESIS: a ratio of 1 means "as steady as his real face", and the steadiness measures separate the faces he
  rejects for warping from the faces he passes. One pass, one put-back reject and one control is a comparison, not a
  line. Test: the group below.
- NOT MEASURED: a REDRAW he rejected, for either fault (the one reject here is a put-back, and the put-back is retired,
  so it is shown beside the group and not counted in it); a longer clip; 720p or 1080p; a head turn; a second take.

**How the group grows (no extra spend, no extra question to him):**

1. `run_draft.py` prints `face_steadiness` with every draft. The numbers go on the post with the draft. They clear
   nothing and fail nothing.
2. He judges the draft as he does now. When his verdict is about the face, record it the same turn:
   `python -I scripts/alter/face_evidence.py add --run <the run_draft folder> --project Meditate --label "<shot, draft>" --verdict pass --words "<his words>"`.
   A rejected face also takes `--fault who` (not him), `steady` (warps, slides) or `other`, read from his words; if
   his words do not say which, that is the one thing to ask.
   A draft he rejects for the action, the scene or the mouth is recorded with `--about action|scene|mouth` and stays
   out of the face group. A wrong row is corrected with `--correct`; the ledger keeps both lines.
3. A draft the tool fails on the uncertified identity line is still shown to him with its number before a re-roll is
   paid for: his verdict on exactly that draft is the evidence the line needs.
4. `face_evidence.py show` prints the group and, per measure, whether every reject for its fault reads above every pass.

**When a line may be proposed** (DECISION of method, a floor written before the group exists so it cannot be fitted to
it): for one measure, at least 5 redraws he passed and 3 redraws he rejected for that measure's fault, from at least 2
takes, with no overlap. (For identity, another person's face in a comparable frame also counts as a "not him".) The
candidate line is the midpoint, shown with the highest pass and the lowest reject. It is then a proposal to Fendi with
the ledger in the same PR (Class C); that PR reviews this floor again with the line, and only it may set
`IDENTITY_GATE_CERTIFIED` or add a steadiness gate. A measure whose rejects overlap its passes cannot be the gate on
its own for that fault; it stays a number on the sheet.

