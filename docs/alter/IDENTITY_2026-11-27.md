## What the readings say

- OBSERVED (one draft): an edit that keeps his face reads close to 0 against its own take (0.021), as does the take's own
  small copy (0.000).
- VERIFIED (one pair): the number does not see a face that slides. The put-back he failed reads *closer* (0.010) than the
  draft he passed. It measures who the face is, not whether it holds still. A face-steadiness measure is still missing.
- VERIFIED: his own face on other takes (other cap, outfit, light, distance) reads 0.28 and 0.30 from this take, and 0.21
  between those two takes. The inherited
  0.25 line (`camera_coverage.py` `identity_max`) therefore sits inside the range of Fendi against Fendi across takes:
  a redraw could move as far from the take as he looks from one take to another and still be under it.
- NOT MEASURED: a face he rejected ("the person was clearly not me in the face", Hyenas, 12 Nov), any other person, a
  generated lookalike. Without one there is no evidence for where "not him" starts.
- PRIOR RECORD, the other way: `scripts/qa/realism_gate.py` notes that on 1 Oct 2026 he judged a generated clip
  "character lock superb" while this model read 0.635 against a reference built from other footage. A high number
  between different clips is therefore not proof of another face either. That script made identity a review signal
  "until it is calibrated against the artist's own verdicts"; this page reaches the same place for ALTER.
- OBSERVED: all faces here are small (a 480p draft, a waist-up frame). HYPOTHESIS: small faces compress the distances;
  test by scoring the same pair from a 4K face crop.

## Decision

DECISION (27 Nov 2026): the identity line is **not certified** (`docs/REPRODUCIBLE_BENCHMARK_SYSTEM.md`: a gate is
derived from evidence, never recalled). In `judge.ts`:

- a number **above** 0.25 still fails the draft, and his words cannot override it in the tool. This is a DECISION that
  fails closed, not a measured boundary: against its own take a kept face read 0.000 to 0.021, so 0.25 on the same
  frames is a large move, but the line itself is as uncertified on this side as on the other. The cost of being wrong
  is a re-roll. If he passes by eye a draft the tool failed on this line, that is the first evidence to move it;
- a number **under** it clears nothing. The draft is CHECK with `faceNeedsHisEye`, the full-resolution plan still needs
  `face_approval` (his own words about that draft's face), and the tool says the line is not certified.

Before this change a number under 0.25 set `redrawCleared` and built the full-resolution call without his words, on a
line nobody had measured.
