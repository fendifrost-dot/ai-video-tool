What the picture shows (OBSERVED on the render and three close frames; Fendi has not judged it at the time of writing):

- **The print is the product's.** Roulette wheel centred on the chest, the yellow table lines, chips, the numbers and
  "PAIR", "CHEVAL", "SIXAINE". Lettering near the hem and edges is garbled or mirrored.
- **The cut is not.** The garment kept the bomber's shape: long sleeves with ribbed cuffs and a ribbed waistband, a
  polo collar on top. The words asked for short sleeves above the elbow and bare forearms; the model filled the old
  sleeves with printed cloth.
- **His hands are damaged on gesturing frames** (print smeared over them, a dark blob on one). That is the matte's
  doing, not evidence about the model's claim to keep hands: they were in the generated region.
- **The kept region is not kept as shot.** The whole frame is regraded (wall warmer, the grey panel on the right goes
  black); 18.5 of 255 on average outside the garment, 13.6 on his face. His face reads as him and his mouth follows
  the take. This matches the vendor's own wording ("only relit and restyled") and our earlier runs.
- **The garment flickers more than the take moves** (1.8x frame-to-frame).
- A stray red patch at his belt on some frames.

Judgement after run 1 (DECISION: nothing changes in the approved route):

- As a swap ENGINE for a patterned product the print fidelity is the best of anything we have tried from one
  product picture. HYPOTHESIS until run against Genjutsu on the same window.
- It does not change a garment's cut inside a matte of the old garment's shape. A short-sleeved shirt over a
  long-sleeved jacket needs a matte and a method that give the model bare arms to draw, or a take shot in short
  sleeves.
- The relight of everything kept means the put-back (his own pixels, proof 0) is not optional after it, and the
  room would need the take's own pixels put back too.
- Not tested: SwitchX 3.0 by name, four references, the web app's Select tool (which would have kept his hands),
  1080p, a second seed.
