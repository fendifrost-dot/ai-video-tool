# Capability scan, 22 Nov 2026: Beeble SwitchX 3.0 (one tool, asked for by Fendi)

Fendi, 22 Nov 2026: "Can you check switch x 3.0 new capabilities it seems to be able to execute all of our clothing /
face swap needs all in one". This is a single-tool scan, not the weekly catalog diff. $0: vendor pages were read,
nothing was generated. Every vendor statement below is a HYPOTHESIS about our footage until a benchmark says otherwise
(`.claude/skills/avt-capability-scan/SKILL.md`).

Pages read on 22 Nov 2026: `docs.beeble.ai/beeble/switchx`, `beeble.ai/research/switchx-3-0` (dated 10 Nov 2026),
`beeble.ai/pricing-cloud`, `developer.beeble.ai` (docs, pricing, the SwitchX start-generation schema), RedShark News
(16 Nov 2026).

## Our own history with SwitchX (VERIFIED, ledgers)

- June 2026 (`AVT_MEMORY_HANDOFF.md`): custom-alpha runs produced "plausible jacket" instead of "this exact jacket";
  the wardrobe lane left SwitchX. Custom matte polarity then: black = keep, white = regenerate.
- 16 Oct 2026, YSL c047 (ledger rows 55, 58, 65-68): background mode gave the best integration of him into a place
  and **changed the wardrobe's colours** (jacket paler, denim bluer).
- 13 Nov 2026, Hyenas (ledger row 29): one relight test, **"not used: it redrew his face"** (lighter skin). That run
  was three days after 3.0's release date, through the API; which version the API served is NOT known.

## Against our needs

| Need | Today's proven method | What 3.0 claims | Judgement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clothing swap on his real take, his face his own pixels | Genjutsu `dress-clip` + `body_only_swap.py` (proof 0) | Select or an uploaded matte keeps face and hands; clothes regenerated from the prompt and references | CANDIDATE as the swap ENGINE only. Two things it does not claim: an exact product (stripe, logo, lettering), and untouched kept pixels (they are relit). Our put-back would still run after it, so proof 0 is still ours to give |
| G1: the same inside a moving scene (train, light sweep) | none passes | camera tracking, moving backgrounds, source-locked subject | CANDIDATE. The closest vendor claim to G1 so far |
| G2: his real performance in a generated moving world without a paste-up | recipe F regenerates him; the composite lane reads pasted on still plates | his footage locked, the world generated round it, light matched in one pass | CANDIDATE. But the world is a generated picture: Fendi, 20 Nov 2026, on the material wall: "DO NOT put me in the front of an AI generated image". His call per shot |
| His face on generated b-roll (he never filmed the action) | recipe F + the tracked face hold | nothing: it keeps a source face, it does not place one | NOT COVERED. No change |
| Mouth | his real footage only | not stated | no lip-sync involved; mouth timing must be measured in any test |

So "all in one" is HYPOTHESIS for two of four needs and false for one. It cannot be marked proven from a vendor page,
and two earlier runs of ours broke the exact rules it would have to keep (wardrobe colour, his face).
