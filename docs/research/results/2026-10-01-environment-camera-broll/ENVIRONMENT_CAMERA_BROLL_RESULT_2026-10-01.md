# Environment / camera / B-roll phase — results (opened 2026-10-01)

Owner: Claude (autonomous, Fendi's AVT-owner directive). Ledger: `docs/LEDGER_ENVIRONMENT_BROLL_BUDGET.md` ($50 ceiling, separate from the parked garment lane). Direction (Fendi, 2026-10-01): the garment lane is parked until models mature (~6 months); this phase is about amazing environments with moving pieces, changing the artist's distance and angle in the camera, repeatable camera/lens functions, and Grok image-to-video B-roll of the artist in the garments with camera movement, FX and scenery — no rapping. Decided together: performance stays real footage in real clothes; B-roll comes from approved anchor stills; the engine lives in the scripts lane first (data-driven from the shot spec), productised through a render worker later.

## 1. Deterministic virtual camera — `scripts/edit/camera_engine.py` + `scripts/edit/lens_presets.json` ($0)

**What it is.** A 2.5D camera over a matted take: the plate is remapped per pixel through a depth-weighted backward map (Depth-Anything-v2-small ONNX, auto-fetched; parallax weight 1 at the performer's plane — read from the plate depth at his feet row — `--near-weight 1.6` in front, `--far-weight 0.35` behind, orbit sign flipping at his plane); the performer is a rigid layer at weight 1 (framing first, then the camera); then a lens post stack from a preset (motion blur from the camera velocity → barrel/pincushion → highlight flare/halation → chromatic aberration → vignette → contrast/saturation → grain). Moves: push, pull, truck, pedestal, crane, orbit, whip_pan, snap_zoom, dolly_zoom, static; each with amount, direction, ease (in_out / in / out / linear / snap), start/end; plus handheld (sum-of-sines noise), keystone/roll and framing (scale, x, y). Inputs come from `composite_environment.py --export-matte` (alpha + decontaminated fg PNGs) so nothing is re-matted. Output: the video, `<base>_camera.json` (spec, lens, per-frame path, overscan, performer plane depth, edge-reveal frames — provenance and what a UI would draw), `<base>_sheet.jpg`. Nothing in it knows a shot or a project; a shot spec's `cameraMovement` / `lens` / `framing` fields can drive it directly.

**Contact render (evidence `camera_contact/`, delivered `camera_engine_contact_8_moves_S11_720p.mp4`).** The same locked 4 s of S11 (frames 0–96 of the 1080x1920 matte) through eight specs: slow push · anamorphic 35; low-angle hero push (keystone 0.07) · anamorphic 35; slider glide · 85 portrait; crane descend · 50 clean; orbit parallax · 85 portrait; whip-pan reframe · handheld 24; dolly zoom · anamorphic 35; locked macro reframe (scale 1.35) · 100 macro. ≈ 1.5 min per 96-frame clip at 1080p on the container CPU. Per-move camera JSONs are in `camera_contact/` (path thinned to every 8th frame). The contact video is silent because the S11 cut carries no audio track (the engine muxes `--audio` with `-ss` from `--range` when the source has one).

**Defects found and fixed (commit `4e6cecd`).** The whip pan with the 24 mm preset crashed: `handheld_24` has `compression 0.9`, `cover_fit` returned a plate smaller than the overscan canvas, the performer-plane depth median was NaN and the DoF level index became INT_MIN. `cover_fit` now reflect-pads when a wide lens undercovers; the auto overscan is capped (`--max-overscan 2.2` — the 0.9 whip asked for 3.98×, a 4× upscale of the plate buys nothing the reflect border does not); an undefined plane depth is a clear `SystemExit`. Separately the contact labels used "·", which the Hershey font renders as "??" — labels are plain ASCII now.

**What the contact video shows.** Push/hero/dolly-zoom read as true camera moves because the strips and floor shift with depth while the performer stays rigid; the orbit shows the strips sliding behind him; the whip's reflect border is visible for a few frames before he lands (expected for a 1080p dev plate — a wider plate or a 4K master crop removes it); the macro reframe crops the cap at scale 1.35 (the framing field, not the engine). The performer never changes distance *relative to the lens* in these — that is the next test (4K master crops for true distance changes; the engine's `framing.scale` already drives apparent size).

## 2. Grok image-to-video B-roll — `supabase/functions/grok-broll-proxy` ($1.546)

**The lane.** New user-JWT edge function (commits `0e3d8eb` → `4e021f6`, deployed by Lovable from main, nothing else touched): `submit` prices the request (`maxCostUsd` gate, dry run), signs an owned still from any project bucket and POSTs xAI `/v1/videos/generations` `{model, prompt, image: {url}, duration, aspect_ratio, resolution}`; `poll` reads `/v1/videos/{id}`, downloads the finished clip to `project-clips/<user>/<project>/grok-broll/<request>.mp4`, inserts a `generated_clip` asset (`metadata_json.lane = grok_broll_image_to_video`, request id, billed cost from usage ticks, prompt version, source still, `shot_label`), idempotent on the request id. Three live defects fixed on the way: xAI needs `image` as an `ImageUrl` struct (422 on a bare string, unbilled); `shot_id` is a uuid column so treatment labels now go to `metadata_json.shot_label` (`shotLabel` field; a non-uuid `shotId` is treated as a label); the gate now prices `grok-imagine-video` at the measured **$0.0703/s** (6 s = $0.422) instead of the $0.05 list rate.

**Test 1 — raw closet anchor (`broll/test1_*`, $0.422).** Prompt: slow push, night rooftop, wet neon concrete, fog, embers, anamorphic. Result: the garment holds for all 6 s (cream body, navy band, gold mark, tie, cap, glasses) and the face stays his — the thing the garment lane could not get from V2V. But frame 0 is the closet, Grok morphs the room into the city inside the first second, and the shoe shelf survives as rooftop debris. A still with the wrong environment makes Grok replace the environment, and it does that as an in-shot transition.

**Test 2 — anchor composited onto the stage plate first (`broll/test2_*`, $0.422).** The anchor was matted (`composite_environment.py` on a 12-frame still video, `--export-matte`) and placed on the treatment's dark mirrored-corridor plate (`broll/anchor_f80_onstage_composite_check.jpg`), uploaded as `heroes/S06/anchor_hook_s11_f0080_onstage.jpg`, and prompted for an orbit + push with pulsing strips, floor fog and dust sparkle, "keep the environment the same, only add motion and atmosphere". Result: the environment stays the stage, the camera orbits and pushes, the strips pulse, fog drifts, the garment and face hold across all 145 frames, no debris. Grok also harmonised the performer's lighting with the plate from frame 2 on (frame 0–1 are the composite's cooler grade — trim or dissolve the first 0.1 s when cutting).

**Test 3 — distance and angle in one shot, 10 s (`broll/test3_*`, $0.702).** Same composited still, prompt: start high and wide with him full body head to shoes, crane down and push in, end low-angle close on face and shoulders. Result: a real distance change inside one clip — full body at frame 0 to a face-and-shoulders close-up at frame 240 — with fog rising, dust sparkle and strip flares building, and the garment, tie, cap, glasses and face holding throughout. Grok read the ending as a close-up from slightly above rather than the requested low angle, so angle is the softer control (distance and the move itself are reliable; the exact end angle is not). Cost is linear in seconds ($0.0703/s), so a 10 s clip that both establishes and lands the close-up is cheaper than two 6 s clips.

**Recipe (general).** Approved anchor still → matte → composite onto the shot's plate (the same plate the performance composite uses) → `grok-broll-proxy` submit with a prompt that names the camera move, the moving pieces and the FX, and tells the model to keep the environment and the garment → poll → asset. ≈ $0.42 per 6 s at 720p. Variables to measure next: 10–15 s clips (cost scales per second), `grok-imagine-video-1.5`, 1080p if the API accepts it, and whether a plate with the strips/fog already in it reduces the model's inventions.

## 4. Realism gate — `scripts/qa/realism_gate.py` (Fendi, 2026-10-01: "reject generic AI video, approve only photoreal")

**Design.** Two tiers, both general. **Tier 1 (deterministic, $0)** measures the clip against REAL FOOTAGE of the same production — the camera masters are the truth for what real looks like. Realism statistics are taken on the performer's face region (so a dark stage and a bright closet compare like for like): grain floor, mid-frequency texture, saturation, specular "sheen"; motion statistics on the whole frame: optical-flow warp error and a flow-unexplained residual; and two "it became something else" measures that turned out to be the ones that matter: **scene drift** (CLIP ViT-B/32 embedding of the frame with the performer painted out — "is this still the same place?") and **identity distance** (ArcFace w600k-r50 embedding of the aligned face against the centroid of the REAL artist's faces from the masters — "is this still him?"). Whole-frame exposure/colour stats are reported but never judged (they measure the grade, not realism). Verdict: REJECT when the scene re-draws itself (drift p95 > 0.28) or the person is someone else (identity distance median > 0.55; REVIEW above 0.40), REVIEW when ≥ 2 statistics sit outside ±2.5 σ of the real footage, PASS otherwise. **Tier 2 (VLM judge, `--judge`)** sends a 10-frame timestamped strip to gpt-6-astra through the existing `astra-visual-review-proxy` with a strict rubric (would a casual phone viewer clock it as AI? tells by category with frame labels, `ai_likelihood`, verdict) — **$0.08–0.11 per clip**, so it can run on every generated clip. The final gate is the stricter of the two.

**Calibration (`realism/`, six clips + two real cuts as reference).** Real S11 cut: tier 1 PASS, judge 0.03 PASS ("reads as photographed footage"). Camera-engine renders (real footage on the plate): identity 0.195–0.205 (him), scene drift 0.14–0.25, REVIEW on grain/warp — the lens presets' grain is far above the real sensor floor and is temporally incoherent (fix: calibrate preset grain to the measured `noise_hf` of the masters). Grok B-roll: test 1 (closet anchor) scene drift 0.306 → REJECT, judge 0.97 ("closet transforms into a neon street with the shoe shelf embedded, subject lighting mismatched, embroidery malformed"); test 2 (stage orbit) identity 0.433 REVIEW, judge 0.78 FAIL (the composited still's head/collar edge smears for the first 0.7 s, lettering strokes rearrange as the camera approaches); test 3 (crane to close-up) identity **0.586 → REJECT**, judge 0.74 FAIL (opening edge smear; the close-up face is "substantially more photographic" but it is not his face by ArcFace); test 4 (lyric literal, below) identity 0.567 → REJECT, judge 0.86 FAIL.

**What the gate says about the lane.** Grok image-to-video keeps the garment but re-draws the face as the camera gets close: identity distance climbs from 0.33 (test 1, medium) to 0.43 (orbit) to 0.57–0.59 (close-ups), against 0.13 for the real cut and ≈ 0.20 for real footage composited on the plate. So, until a provider holds identity, the recipe is medium/wide only (no push into a close-up), and the composited still must enter clean: the 720p RVM still-matte leaves a soft head/collar edge the judge catches in every first second — render the still at the master's resolution with a hard matte, or trim the first 0.75 s where Grok has already harmonised it. Both are mechanical; the second is already true for any cut on the beat.

## 5. Bringing every lyric to life — `supabase/functions/lyric-visualizer-proxy` (Fendi, 2026-10-01: "the creativity of the treatment center is terrible")

**Where the creativity actually lives.** The treatment is drafted by Control Center's `ai-draft-treatment` (through `proxy-provider-call`, a hard-lock boundary for Claude) and the in-app Creative Director planner is the deterministic mock (beat grid + rules, no LLM, no lyrics) — which is why the YSL draft is tasteful stock luxury B-roll (champagne pour, cash stack, rotating diamond) with each lyric pinned as a `LYRIC:` note rather than made into a picture. The new proxy is the missing creative layer inside AVT: per lyric line, three concepts — **literal** (the words made physically real), **surreal** (the metaphor pushed past reality, staged like a camera witnessed it), **performance** (the artist delivering it with an environment/camera idea) — each with camera, motion/FX, an honest realism risk against the renderer's limits, and a B-roll prompt that reuses the locked hero description verbatim and honours the project's locked rules. One xAI chat call per line in parallel (a whole-hook call hit the gateway's 150 s idle timeout), strict JSON schema, cost gate, dry run. grok-4.6, **$0.169 for the eight hook lines** (24 concepts; `lyrics/HOOK_CONCEPTS_V1.md`).

**Examples it produced.** "More cameras in the whip than a camera crew" → *Lenses in the Glass*: round lenses press out of the black mirror panels as if a crew shoots him through two-way glass. "My money talk for me and it be rambling too" → *Money Double in the Glass*: his reflection is a loosely assembled figure of drifting bills that never stops spilling. "All this ice around me, need a Canada Goose" → *Private Blizzard* (coin-sized ice shards orbit him at chest height, never falling) and *Black Ice Spreads* (the wet floor flash-freezes toward his shoes, frost climbs the strips, one breath of vapour). "YSL I wear em like they white ones" → *Corridor Goes White* (the corridor blooms to a white void and he is the only colour left).

**Test 4 — one concept rendered end to end (`broll/test4_*`, $0.562 + $0.11 judge).** *Black Ice Spreads*, 8 s from the v2 composited anchor (tighter matte, lighter grade). The idea lands on screen — ice crystallises across the floor, frost up the strips, breath vapour, slow push — and the **gate rejected it**: identity 0.567 (Grok re-drew a younger, slimmer face in the push), judge 0.86 (opening edge smear, the floor "regenerating" rather than freezing, the strips turning into luminous ribbons). That is the system working as Fendi asked: the creative layer proposes, the renderer renders, the gate refuses what a viewer would clock — and the reasons are specific enough to act on (keep the camera medium, enter with a clean still, prompt the ice as a surface change not a particle spawn).

## 6. Higgsfield (Fendi's realism benchmark)

Higgsfield has a developer API (`api.higgsfield.ai`, key id:secret auth, async queue + poll, ≥ 7-day output retention): its own **DoP** image-to-video with named camera-motion presets (`/v1/image2video/dop`, `motions[]`, `getMotions()`), **Soul** character images, and a routed catalogue of third-party video models (Kling, Seedance, Wan, MiniMax, Veo, Grok) at published per-second rates ($0.042–0.20/s; DoP ≈ $0.14–0.57 per 5 s by tier). Pay-as-you-go in dollars, failed requests free, 20 concurrent per key. Nothing in the docs claims identity preservation; whether its DoP holds the artist's face under a push is exactly what the identity gate will measure. Needs an account, a top-up and a key (`HIGGSFIELD_API_KEY` = `key_id:key_secret` in AVT's Lovable Cloud secrets) — Fendi's action; the B-roll proxy gets a `provider` switch the moment the key exists.

## 3. Open and next

1. **Plate quality.** The dev plate is candidate #2 of the hook plate sheet upscaled 504x896 → 1080x1920; the production plates were never stored. Generate the hook plate at native 1080x1920 (or larger, for overscan) through the product's image proxy and keep it under `plates/` in project-references — the camera engine and the B-roll recipe both need it.
2. **Distance changes.** The masters are 4K vertical; crops of the 4K master through the same matte path give true distance changes without the engine's upscale. Add `--source-crop` to the matte export and a `framing.distance` key that maps to it.
3. **Shot-spec fields.** `framing`, `cameraAngle`, `lens` are empty on every shot; the engine's spec object is the schema to adopt (move / lens / angle / framing / handheld / focus / grade) so a shot row drives a render with no hand-written JSON.
4. **Render worker.** The engine needs a GPU-less CPU box with ffmpeg + onnxruntime; an edge function cannot run it. Productise once the spec fields exist.
5. **B-roll sweep through the gate.** Medium/wide lyric concepts from the hook sheet, each rendered from a clean still and gated (tier 1 + the $0.10 judge) before any cut; identity failures are the provider comparison (Grok vs Higgsfield DoP vs Kling) the moment a second key exists.
6. **Grain calibration.** Lens presets' grain and motion blur set from the masters' measured `noise_hf`/`warp_err` instead of taste.
7. **Still ingest.** Composite the anchor at the master's resolution with a hard matte (or trim 0.75 s) so the judge's first-second edge tell disappears.
