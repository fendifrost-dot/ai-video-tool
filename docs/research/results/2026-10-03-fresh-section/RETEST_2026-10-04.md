## 5. Built for this (on main, published `0aef034`)

- `src/lib/storyboard/takeCheck.ts` — lip timing and framing of a restaged clip against its take; the bar for
  agreement grows as the comparison shortens (`minCorr`); a face is read only where it is lit enough to read.
- `src/lib/media/faceSeries.ts` — his face on every frame (MediaPipe face landmarker, loaded on first use from its
