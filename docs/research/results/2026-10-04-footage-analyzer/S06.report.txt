  hard constraints:
    · [composition] Leave the performer's place clear: his eye-line sits at 51 % across and 34 % down the frame. Put nothing of interest there, and nothing that has to be read behind his head and shoulders.
    · [composition] He moves across 41 % of the frame and 3 % down it. Keep that band clear of anything the eye has to follow.
    · [camera] The camera does not move. The background must be locked off too — any drift in it will read as the room sliding behind a man who is standing still.
    · [light] Mean brightness is 0.53 of full scale. Build the background to that, not brighter: he cannot be relit.
    · [colour] The take carries a colour cast (r 0.53 g 0.51 b 0.44). Grade the background to it rather than correcting him.
    · [colour] The file is tagged arib-std-b67. A background generated in ordinary sRGB will not match it until one of the two is converted, and converting him is what loses his skin.
    · [focus] The background must be delivered at 1080 × 1920 or larger. Anything smaller has to be scaled up into him.
    · [reframe] Do not reframe wider or lower than the take. There is no footage of his legs or feet; a background that reveals them will reveal nothing.
  preferences:
    · [composition] The take frames him thigh up. A background built for a fuller figure will not match: there is no footage of the rest of him.
    · [light] The brighter side of the picture is above. Light the background from the same side; a background keyed from the opposite side is the single most visible mismatch in a composite.
    · [light] His light reads medium. Match that: a background lit flatter than he is will look pasted behind him.
    · [focus] The background is about as sharp as he is: a defocused plate will read as a different lens.
    · [separation] A matte is workable but not free; keep the background away from his edges in brightness and detail.
    · [floor] The frame ends above his feet, so no floor contact and no contact shadow has to be matched — one fewer thing for the background to get wrong.
