# Coverage — movement and angle changes as the norm (built, tested, adjusted 2026-10-02)

Fendi: "music videos are almost always filled with lots of camera movement and switching of angles … I would have to do each performance shot 3–6× to get the different camera angle cuts … this should be the norm for the tool, not the exception." Then: "Let's build it all. Then test and make adjustments."

## What was built (plan Part E)

* **`config/coverage_presets.json`** — per section (verse / hook / bridge / default): the move vocabulary with odds, how often to cut (bars), the share of cuts that are generated angles, the angle sentences for the Seedance lane, and the transition on the 1. Rules: static ≤ 12 % of performance time, longest static run ≤ 4 s, no move or framing repeats on consecutive cuts, pushes and pulls alternate, framing rotates medium → close → wide → medium-close.
* **`scripts/edit/coverage.py plan`** — every performance slot is cut on the bar grid (section from the timed lyric lines), each cut draws a move deterministically (seeded by the slot id) under the rules; a share of cuts becomes a generated-angle request (`angle_requests.json`, for `run_world_batch.py --route seedance_ref`); outputs `shotspecs_coverage.json` + `renders_coverage.json` for the assembler.
* **`scripts/edit/coverage.py render`** — a 2D virtual camera over the finished slot clip (zoom / pan / roll / handheld from `camera_engine.camera_path`, the lens stack from `lens_presets.json`), or the 2.5D camera (`camera_engine.py`, depth parallax) when the slot has a matte export. The move spans the sub-slot's window inside the slot clip so the assembler cuts it with the slot's own `masterStart`.
* **`scripts/qa/coverage_qa.py`** — moving share, longest static run, consecutive repeats, cut cadence per section; PASS/FAIL against the presets.
* **App** — `applyCoverageDefaults` (`src/lib/treatment/coverage.ts`, tested, mirrors the JSON by test) gives every performance card a move and a framing at treatment time; the director's own choice always wins.
* **`config/treatment_templates/motion_story_v1.json`** + a seed `prompt_templates` row — the story-led motion template Fendi supplied, turned into a per-scene motion contract (entrance / primary / secondary / exit), a camera line on every scene, object-based transitions (`match_cut` preset added) and a sound cue; generator wiring lands with B4.

## Adjustments made from the test

1. **Whip pans are transitions, not in-shot moves.** As a move on a one-bar cut a whip arrives from off-frame and shows the frame edge (`bar3_hook_region_2fps.jpg`, last frames); as a transition it straddles the cut and never does. Removed from the hook vocabulary (a fast snap-eased truck replaces it); `whip_left` stays as the hook's transition on the 1.
2. **The 2D orbit is a stand-in.** A lateral drift with a touch of roll reads as a dutch drift, not an orbit; roll reduced (2.0 → 1.2° per unit). The true orbit is the 2.5D camera, which needs a matte export per take (`composite_environment.py --export-matte`) — the next $0 step for the hero slots.
3. The lens stack grades on top of the plate-aware composite (vignette, grain, contrast); mild on these takes, but the finishing pass (`grade_to_bank.py`) should run after coverage, not before, so it is applied once.
