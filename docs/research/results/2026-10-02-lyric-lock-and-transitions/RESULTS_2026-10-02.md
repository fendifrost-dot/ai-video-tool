## 1. The measured reason the sample was off the lyrics

## 3. Transitions engine (plan C1, on main)

`scripts/edit/transitions.py` + `config/transition_presets.json`: cut · crossfade · dip to black/white · flash · whip pan (directional blur ramp + push) · zoom punch · speed ramp (the outgoing tail compressed from its handle) · strobe on sixteenths · luma wipe (the incoming shot arrives where it is brightest) · glitch (block displacement + channel split) · light leak · film burn. Durations in beats; `side` says which shot gives up frames; the frame budget of every slot is kept so the song clock never moves. `assemble_section.py` renders each slot with 1 s handles and applies the card's `transitionIn` (a preset name, or a type with beats/params; the legacy types still resolve). Verified on the sample: 43.279 s out for 43.2787 expected with ten different transitions; audition cut delivered (`TRANSITIONS_audition_bars24-46.mp4`); `transitions_contact.jpg` shows the whip, luma wipe, glitch and leak across the S04 → atelier cut. `transitions.py --audition` renders the two seconds around one cut for picking (the C3 page's primitive).
