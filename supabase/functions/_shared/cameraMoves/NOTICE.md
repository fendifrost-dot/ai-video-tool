# Camera-move library — third-party notice

The move names, descriptions, camera numbers, poster stills, and the pill-clip
hashes in this folder come from:

AI filmmaking camera control with Seedance 2.5 and three.js
https://github.com/sirioberati/ai-filmmaking-camera-control-seedance-2-5
Copyright (c) 2026 Sirio Berati
Commit 602aa2348b0ae67589ce533fff571adced724811

That work is MIT licensed. The license text follows. AVT does not use Tein.
The three.js pill clips themselves are not committed here (about 124 MB).
`scripts/camera-moves/render-references.mjs` copies those upstream clips or
rasterizes a blockout from the same move names.

The prompt block order follows `docs/prompt-anatomy.md` in that repository.
Wording that would turn the capsule into a person is not used. AVT's default
is an environment plate: his face and mouth, on a performance, stay the
original footage.
